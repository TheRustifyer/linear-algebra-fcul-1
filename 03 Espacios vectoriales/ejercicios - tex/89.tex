\setcounter{enumi}{88}
\item {\bfseries\boldmath
Discuss, in terms of the real parameter $\lambda$, the dimension of the following vector subspace of the real vector space $\RR^4$:
}

\[
V=\left\langle(1,2,1,\lambda),(0,\lambda,-1,1),(-\lambda,0,-1,1),(-2,-4,-2,\lambda^2)\right\rangle.
\]

\begin{answer}
Use the generators as rows of
\[
 R_\lambda=\begin{bmatrix}
 1&2&1&\lambda\\0&\lambda&-1&1\\-\lambda&0&-1&1\\-2&-4&-2&\lambda^2
 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
The dimension of their span is the rank of this row matrix.
Apply $F_4\gets F_4+2F_1$ and expand along row $4$:
\[
 \det R_\lambda=\lambda(\lambda+2)
 \det\begin{bmatrix}1&2&1\\0&\lambda&-1\\-\lambda&0&-1\end{bmatrix}
 =\lambda^2(\lambda+1)(\lambda+2).
\]
For $\lambda\notin\{0,-1,-2\}$, the rank is $4$.
For $\lambda=0$, row $3$ equals row $2$, row $4$ equals $-2$ times row $1$,
and rows $1,2$ are independent. The rank is $2$.
For $\lambda=-1$, the minor in rows $1,2,4$ and columns $1,2,4$ has determinant
\[
 \det\begin{bmatrix}1&2&-1\\0&-1&1\\-2&-4&1\end{bmatrix}=1.
\]
Thus the rank is at least $3$ and, as the full determinant is zero, is exactly $3$.
For $\lambda=-2$, the leading $3\times3$ minor has determinant
$\lambda(\lambda+1)=2$, so the rank is also $3$.
Consequently
\[
 \boxed{\dim V=\begin{cases}
 2,&\lambda=0,\\3,&\lambda\in\{-1,-2\},\\
 4,&\lambda\notin\{0,-1,-2\}.
 \end{cases}}
\]
\end{answer}

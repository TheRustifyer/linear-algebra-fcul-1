\setcounter{enumi}{81}
\item {\bfseries\boldmath
Determine a basis for each of the subspaces in Exercise 64.
}

\begin{answer}
Only $E_1$ and $E_4$ are subspaces.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[$\mathbf{(a)}$] In $E_1$, the equations give $a=\frac52c$, $b=c-d$.
Hence
\[
 ax^3+bx^2+cx+d=c\left(\tfrac52x^3+x^2+x\right)+d(-x^2+1).
\]
A basis is $\bigl(\frac52x^3+x^2+x,\ -x^2+1\bigr)$.
If a linear combination of these two polynomials is zero, its coefficient of
$x$ and then its constant term give both coefficients zero.
\item[$\mathbf{(d)}$] A basis of $E_4$ is $(x^3,x^2,1)$.
These polynomials generate its elements by definition and are independent
by comparison of coefficients.
\end{enumerate}
The remaining sets in Exercise 64 are not vector subspaces, so they have no
vector space basis with the inherited operations.
\end{answer}

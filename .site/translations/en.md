# Linear Algebra and Analytic Geometry

**Institution:** Faculty of Sciences, University of Lisbon (FCUL)  
**Academic year:** 2026/27  
**Official course title:** *Álgebra Linear e Geometria Analítica*


This map follows the seven units in FCUL's 2026/27 programme. The programme for 2025/26 gives more detail on several units; its additional topics are included below where useful.

## 1. Matrices and real and complex linear systems

Basic matrix operations; elementary transformations and elementary matrices; rank; Gauss–Jordan elimination; matrix inversion; solving linear systems.

## 2. Determinants

Definition and computation; Laplace expansion; properties; Cramer's rule; applications to solving linear systems and inverting matrices.

## 3. Abstract vector spaces

Vector spaces and subspaces; linear combinations, spanning sets, and linear independence; bases, dimension, and coordinates; row and column spaces; sums and direct sums; change of basis.

## 4. Linear transformations

Definition and matrix representation; kernel and image; injectivity and surjectivity; isomorphisms; change of basis.

## 5. Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and diagonalization

Eigenspaces; characteristic polynomial; algebraic and geometric multiplicities; diagonalization criteria; symmetric matrices.

## 6. Euclidean spaces

Inner products, norms, angles, and orthogonal projections; Cauchy–Schwarz inequality; orthogonal bases and Gram–Schmidt; orthogonal complements; cross product in $\mathbb R^3$.

## 7. Quadratic forms

Definition; positive-definite, negative-definite, and indefinite forms; classification using eigenvalues and the leading principal-minor criterion.

\item \textbf{Show that if $A\in M_n(K)$ is invertible,
    then $A^{-1}$ is also invertible and} $ (A^{-1})^{-1}=A$.
        
    \begin{answer}
        Since $A$ is invertible, by definition,

        $$
        AA^{-1}=A^{-1}A=I_n.
        $$

        Hence, $A$ is an inverse of $A^{-1}$.
        Therefore, $A^{-1}$ is invertible and

        $$
        \boxed{(A^{-1})^{-1}=A}.
        $$
    \end{answer}

\item Consider the following system of linear equations in the unknowns
$x$, $y$, $z$, $w$:

$$
(S):
\begin{cases}
x+2y-2z-w=0\\
2x+y-z+4w=0\\
-x-y+z-w=0
\end{cases}
$$

Give a system of linear equations whose associated homogeneous system
is $(S)$ and for which $(2,-1,0,-1)$ is a solution.

\begin{answer}
    The coefficient matrix of $(S)$ is
    $$
    A=
    \begin{bmatrix}
    1&2&-2&-1\\
    2&1&-1&4\\
    -1&-1&1&-1
    \end{bmatrix}.
    $$

    We seek a system $AX=B$ for which
    $$
    X=
    \begin{bmatrix}
    2\\-1\\0\\-1
    \end{bmatrix}
    $$
    is a solution.

    Hence we must choose $B=AX$.

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    B
    &=
    \begin{bmatrix}
    1&2&-2&-1\\
    2&1&-1&4\\
    -1&-1&1&-1
    \end{bmatrix}
    \begin{bmatrix}
    2\\-1\\0\\-1
    \end{bmatrix}\\[6pt]
    &=
    \begin{bmatrix}
    2-2+1\\
    4-1-4\\
    -2+1+1
    \end{bmatrix}\\[6pt]
    &=
    \begin{bmatrix}
    1\\-1\\0
    \end{bmatrix}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore, the required system is
    $$
    \boxed{
    \begin{cases}
    x+2y-2z-w=1\\
    2x+y-z+4w=-1\\
    -x-y+z-w=0
    \end{cases}
    }
    $$

    {\color{red}
    \textbf{Note:} The associated homogeneous system is obtained by replacing the
    independent terms with zero while preserving the coefficient matrix.

    Therefore, choosing $B=AX$ guarantees that the given vector is a solution
    of the new system.}
\end{answer}

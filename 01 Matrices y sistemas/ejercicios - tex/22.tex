\item \textbf{Let $A_1,A_2,\ldots,A_n$ be invertible
    square matrices of the same order. Express
    $(A_1A_2\cdots A_n)^{-1}$ in terms of
    $A_1^{-1},A_2^{-1},\ldots,A_n^{-1}$.} \\[0.5em]
        
    \begin{answer}
        Using $(AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1}$ successively,

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        (A_1A_2\cdots A_n)^{-1}
            &=A_n^{-1}(A_1A_2\cdots A_{n-1})^{-1}\\
            &=A_n^{-1}A_{n-1}^{-1}
            (A_1A_2\cdots A_{n-2})^{-1}\\
            &=\cdots\\
            &=\boxed{A_n^{-1}A_{n-1}^{-1}\cdots A_2^{-1}A_1^{-1}}.
        \end{aligned}
        $$
    \end{answer}

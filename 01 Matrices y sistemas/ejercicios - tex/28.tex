\item {\bfseries\boldmath
Compute the inverse of the matrix
}

\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&i\\0&2i&-1\\2&-4&i\end{bmatrix}.
\]

\begin{answer}
For $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&i\\0&2i&-1\\2&-4&i\end{bmatrix}$, apply to
$[A\mid I_3]$ the operations
$F_3\gets F_3-2F_1$, $F_2\gets-\frac i2F_2$,
$F_3\gets F_3+4F_2$, $F_3\gets-iF_3$. This gives
\[
\left[\begin{array}{rrr|rrr}
 1&0&i&1&0&0\\0&1&i/2&0&-i/2&0\\0&0&1&2i&-2&-i
\end{array}\right].
\]
Finally, $F_1\gets F_1-iF_3$ and $F_2\gets F_2-\frac i2F_3$ give
\[
 A^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}3&2i&-1\\1&i/2&-1/2\\2i&-2&-i\end{bmatrix}.
\]
\end{answer}
